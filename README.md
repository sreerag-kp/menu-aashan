## 🚀 How to Install on Android

1. **Download APK**: Tap the download button above on your Android phone or tablet.
2. **Open File**: Once download finishes, open your notification tray or go to your device's **Files / Downloads** app and tap `MenuAashan-Owner-POS.apk`.
3. **Allow Installation**:
   - If prompted with *"For your security, your phone is not allowed to install unknown apps from this source"*:
   - Tap **Settings** ➔ Toggle on **Allow from this source** ➔ Press the back button.
4. **Install**: Tap **Install** and wait 5–10 seconds.
5. **Launch & Login**: Open **MenuAashan**, sign in with your restaurant owner credentials, and start managing your digital menu and live orders!

## ✨ Key Features

### 📋 Live Menu & Category Management
- Create, edit, and reorganize categories with instant cloud sync.
- Toggle dish availability (**In Stock** / **Sold Out**) in real time without refreshing.
- Mark items with dietary labels: **Veg 🟢**, **Non-Veg 🔴**, **Spicy 🌶️**, or **Chef's Special ⭐**.
- Upload rich HD dish photos backed by Cloudinary CDN.

### 🔍 AI Menu Card Scanner
- Snap a photo of any physical paper menu card.
- The built-in AI parser automatically extracts dish names, descriptions, and prices directly into your digital menu catalogue.

### 💳 Instant Razorpay Subscriptions
- Upgrade to Haute Cuisine Pro with automated Weekly or Monthly subscriptions.
- Seamless integration with Razorpay UPI (GPay, PhonePe, Paytm, BHIM) and Credit/Debit Cards.

### 🔒 Enterprise-Grade Security
- Single-device active session watchdog preventing unauthorized account sharing.
- Real-time account status synchronization: automatic lockouts on account suspension or deletion.
- Role-Based Access Control protecting restaurant data.

## 🛠️ System Requirements

- **Operating System**: Android 8.0 (API Level 26) or higher.
- **RAM**: 2 GB minimum (3 GB+ recommended for smooth camera scanning).
- **Storage**: ~70 MB free internal storage.
- **Internet**: Active Wi-Fi or Mobile Data (4G/5G) required for real-time order and menu synchronization.
